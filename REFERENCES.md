# References and source note

These notes were developed as an original study guide from the subjects in the user's supplied security-operations, CTI, intelligence-lifecycle, data-collection, OSINT, standards, analytic-model, TIP, dissemination, and INE threat-hunting course files. The source documents themselves are not redistributed. The separate exam-answer document was not used or reproduced; the review questions in this repository are original.

The guide summarizes concepts in new wording, adds defensive learning structure and original exercises, and links to primary documentation for further study. It is not a substitute for the full courses, current standards, official certification content, or legal advice.

## CTI and intelligence sharing

- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) ? risk-management framework and terminology.
- [NIST SP 800-150: Guide to Cyber Threat Information Sharing](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/150/final) ? planning and managing threat-information sharing.
- [OASIS CTI documentation](https://oasis-open.github.io/cti-documentation/) ? STIX and TAXII documentation.
- [FIRST Traffic Light Protocol](https://www.first.org/tlp/) ? current TLP definitions and handling guidance.
- [MISP project documentation](https://www.misp-project.org/documentation/) ? open-source threat-information sharing platform.

## Analysis and behavior models

- [MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) ? current adversary behavior knowledge base.
- [Lockheed Martin Cyber Kill Chain](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/capabilities/cyber/cyber-kill-chain.html) ? intrusion-stage model.
- [CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) ? evidence of vulnerabilities exploited in the wild; use alongside local exposure and risk.

## Defensive operations and hunting

- [NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3: Incident Response Recommendations and Considerations](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final) ? incident response within cybersecurity risk management.
- [Microsoft Sysinternals Sysmon](https://learn.microsoft.com/sysinternals/downloads/sysmon) ? endpoint event generation; configuration determines collected events.
- [Wireshark documentation](https://www.wireshark.org/docs/) ? packet-analysis reference.
- [Splunk Search Reference](https://docs.splunk.com/Documentation/Splunk/latest/SearchReference) and [Elastic documentation](https://www.elastic.co/guide/index.html) ? query and platform documentation; field schemas vary.

## Certification study

- [INE certifications](https://ine.com/learning/certifications) ? official, current certification information. Search INE's current catalog for eCTHP course and candidate guidance. Certification requirements and objectives can change.
