# Glossary

- **ATT&CK:** MITRE knowledge base of adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures grounded in documented behavior.
- **Confidence:** A stated degree of belief in an analytic judgment, supported by evidence and assumptions.
- **CTI:** Cyber threat intelligence; evaluated and contextualized analysis intended to inform a defensive decision.
- **Data source:** A system or collection point producing evidence, such as EDR, DNS, identity, or firewall telemetry.
- **Enrichment:** Adding attributes to an observable or event, such as provider or registration context.
- **Hypothesis:** A testable statement describing expected evidence within a defined scope and period.
- **Indicator / IoC:** An observable that may be associated with activity of interest, interpreted in context.
- **Intelligence requirement:** A decision-linked question that directs collection and analysis.
- **OSINT:** Intelligence derived from publicly available information collected and analyzed for a defined purpose.
- **PIR:** Priority Intelligence Requirement; a high-priority question directing an intelligence effort.
- **Provenance:** The origin, collection method, and chain of handling for a data point or claim.
- **STIX:** A structured language for representing cyber threat information.
- **TAXII:** A protocol for exchanging cyber threat information, often STIX content.
- **TLP:** Traffic Light Protocol, a sharing/handling convention for information.
- **TTP:** Tactics, techniques, and procedures used to describe adversary behavior at different levels of detail.
- **TIP:** Threat intelligence platform used to manage, enrich, correlate, and share CTI.
