## Revision checklist

Before an exam or practical assessment, confirm that you can:

- Explain how CTI differs from raw data, an alert, and attribution.
- Describe planning, collection, processing, analysis, dissemination, and feedback.
- Evaluate sources and indicators, including freshness, provenance, relevance, and confidence.
- Distinguish the Cyber Kill Chain from ATT&CK and use each appropriately.
- Explain what network and endpoint sources can show and what they cannot.
- Write a testable hypothesis, define scope and alternatives, and handle inconclusive results.
- Escalate suspected active incidents through the established process.

Use the official INE course and certification guidance for any current domain weighting or exam requirements.
