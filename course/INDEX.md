# Course map

This course moves from intelligence questions to evidence-led hunting and operational feedback. Each module has learning outcomes and practical review prompts.

## Part I - Cyber threat intelligence

1. [SOC and CTI foundations](01-soc-and-cti-foundations.md) - security operations, roles, and the intelligence value chain.
2. [Intelligence types and indicators](02-intelligence-types-and-indicators.md) - strategic/operational/tactical/technical intelligence, IoCs, and indicator limits.
3. [The intelligence lifecycle](03-intelligence-lifecycle.md) - direction, collection, processing, analysis, dissemination, and feedback.
4. [Data collection and quality](04-data-collection-and-quality.md) - internal/external sources, coverage, provenance, and evaluation.
5. [OSINT method](05-osint-method.md) - ethical collection, verification, and exposure management.
6. [Sharing standards](06-sharing-standards.md) - STIX, TAXII, TLP, and interoperability.
7. [Analytical models](07-analytical-models.md) - Cyber Kill Chain, ATT&CK, courses of action, and structured analysis.
8. [Threat intelligence platforms](08-threat-intelligence-platforms.md) - TIP capabilities and governance.
9. [Dissemination and feedback](09-dissemination-and-feedback.md) - audience-based products and outcome measures.

## Part II - Threat hunting and eCTHP-focused practice

10. [Threat hunting fundamentals](10-threat-hunting-fundamentals.md) - proactive hunts, prerequisites, and maturity.
11. [Hunt strategies and hypotheses](11-hunt-strategies-and-hypotheses.md) - structured, unstructured, and situational hunts.
12. [Intelligence-driven hunting](12-intelligence-driven-hunting.md) - turn reporting into falsifiable hypotheses and data plans.
13. [Network hunting](13-network-hunting.md) - flow, DNS, HTTP(S), packet evidence, and baselines.
14. [Endpoint hunting](14-endpoint-hunting.md) - process, identity, persistence, and Windows telemetry.
15. [Analysis, detection, and reporting](15-analysis-detection-and-reporting.md) - validate, scope, hand off, and improve.
16. [Integrated capstone](16-integrated-capstone.md) - connect CTI, endpoint, and network investigation.
17. [eCTHP-focused study crosswalk](17-ecthp-study-crosswalk.md) - align course topics to defensive skills without claiming an official exam blueprint.
18. [Glossary](18-glossary.md) - key terms.

## Learning outcomes

By the end, a learner should be able to define an intelligence requirement, select fit-for-purpose sources, qualify evidence, explain analytic uncertainty, frame an ATT&CK-informed hunt, identify telemetry gaps, distinguish signal from benign variation, and communicate useful next steps to the right audience.

## How to use the notes

For each module, write a short answer to its review prompts before moving on. For technical exercises, state the target and authorization, time window, data sources, expected evidence, and stop conditions before querying data. Use current product documentation because tools, frameworks, and certification blueprints change.
